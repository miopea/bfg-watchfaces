Make a watch face that is yours.

BFG Watch Faces is a design studio for Wear OS dials. Choose a style, set your
colours, and watch the dial redraw as you move each slider. When you like what
you see, give it a name — and that name is what the face is called from then on.

SEVEN ENGINE-TURNED PATTERNS
Rosette, barleycorn, clous de Paris, lattice, sunburst, botanical and knotwork —
the guilloché patterns traditionally cut into fine watch dials. They are drawn
fresh from your settings every time, not picked from a short list of presets, so
two people almost never end up with the same dial.

FOUR QUIET SURFACES
Grain, brushed, carbon and linen, for when a pattern is more than you want.

EVERY LINE IS ENGRAVED, NOT PRINTED
Each stroke is drawn three times: a light pass, a dark pass, and a fine one
between them. That is what gives a real engraved dial its depth, and it is what
gives this one its depth too.

COLOURS THAT ARE ACTUALLY YOURS
Dial and ink are set independently. So are the pattern's scale and density, the
sheen across the surface, and the shadow at the rim. Nothing is locked.

COMPLICATIONS WHERE YOU WANT THEM
Steps, heart rate, battery, date, day of week, world clock, your next event,
sunrise and sunset, unread notifications, a favourite contact, or a shortcut to
an app — placed in the slots around the dial.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADVERTISING, NO TRACKING
There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to opt out of. The app collects no
personal data and contains no analytics or advertising libraries at all.
