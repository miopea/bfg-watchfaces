Privacy Policy

BFG Watch Faces (com.bfg.watchfaces) — last updated 29 August 2026

The short version

BFG Watch Faces does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK that reports anything about you. Everything you make stays on your own devices.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the watch faces you design — the parameters that describe them, such as pattern, colours, layout and which complications you have chosen. On the watch, it also stores a single record of whether you allowed it to switch your active watch face.

All of this is written to the app's own private storage on your phone or watch. It is not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions, and why each exists

  • Internet — declared by the phone app but currently unused. The app makes no network requests of any kind today. It is declared for a community face catalogue that is not yet built. When that ships, this policy will be updated before it does, not after.
  • Notifications (watch) — used once, to tell you a face has arrived and to let you tap through to the permission request below. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Change the active watch face (watch) — lets the app put a face you made onto your watch face for you. You are asked once, you may decline, and declining only means you switch faces yourself by long-pressing the dial.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, regardless of age.

Data deletion

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing held elsewhere to delete. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. If a future version sends anything anywhere, this policy will say so, and will explain how to remove it.

Changes to this policy

This policy is kept in the app's public source repository, so its full history is visible at github.com/miopea/bfg-watchfaces. Material changes will be made here before the behaviour they describe ships.
